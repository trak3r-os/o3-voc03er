# TRACT (V1), the fifth mode: the vocoder runs the carrier alone and the tract unit (a vocal tract, o3-sdk's extras/tract) speaks it. F1 steps to it
# (the first tap takes the focus), the state line says `voc_mode=tract`, a chord held sounds through the tube, and F1 round again is BANDS. The
# simulator shows the screen and that the chord sounds; whether it sounds like a mouth is by ear on the unit. The tube's knobs have no screen
# yet (V2): the vowel sweep is the console's (`tract vowel <0..1>`) and is tested in tests/tract_mode_test.cpp, a scenario has no console line.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/tract.txt --out <dir>   (the dir must exist)
record on
input tone 220 -12
paddown 36
paddown 40
paddown 43
wait 600
snap 01_bands
expect state voc_mode=bands
tap f1
tap f1
tap f1
tap f1
tap f1
wait 800
snap 02_tract
expect state voc_mode=tract
wait 600
snap 03_tract_held
padup 36
padup 40
padup 43
wait 400
paddown 36
wait 600
snap 04_tract_one_note
padup 36
wait 300
tap f1
wait 800
snap 05_bands_again
expect state voc_mode=bands
