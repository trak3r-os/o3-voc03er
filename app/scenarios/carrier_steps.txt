# The per-step carrier switch (V5c): four notes written into the pattern, a carrier set on three steps (the PATTERN screen's CARRIER lane: SY synth,
# NZ noise, L1..L4 loops; SHIFT + LEFT / RIGHT move the cursor), the transport started with SHIFT + PLAY so the bar runs, and the cells
# showing the tags. The simulator's input is synthetic: this shows the screen and that the switch path runs; how each carrier sounds
# and the click at a switch on a real source are the unit's to say.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/carrier_steps.txt --out <dir>   (the dir must exist)
record on
wait 200
tap pattern  # the PATTERN screen: with REC on the palette pads (the lower two rows) write the cursor's step and move it on
wait 100
tap rec
pad 24
pad 27
pad 31
pad 27
snap 01_four_notes
press shift
tap left
tap left
tap left
tap left
release shift
tap instr  # the CARRIER lane: SYNTH on step 1, a step skipped, NOISE on step 3, SLOT 1 on step 4
pad 25
press shift
tap right
release shift
pad 26
pad 28
snap 02_carriers_set
expect state carriers=3
press shift
tap play
release shift
wait 1200
snap 03_running
expect state playing=1
wav carrier_steps
press shift
tap play
release shift
wait 200
expect state playing=0
press shift
tap config
snap 04_help
release shift
