# SAMPLE's lower pad rows play the notes palette (SP1; Q7 first made them PERFORM's rows), the upper two rows stay the slot's slices. The palette is PATTERN's F6 TOP PADS,
# one of six pairs in order: NOTE+TRIAD (A NOTE, B TRIAD: the default), NOTE, TRIAD, 7TH, OPEN, POWER; SAMPLE has no key for it (F1 is the slot), and the right panel
# names the pair. Two rows of the same kind play the same notes an octave apart (A above B). Then PERFORM goes to TRACT (A NOTE, B TONGUE, C MOUTH) and SAMPLE still
# plays its own notes palette (11, 12). The WAV carries the notes; the unit's feel and the keys' LEDs by hand are for the unit.
