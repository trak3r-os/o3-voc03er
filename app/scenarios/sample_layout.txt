# NOTE and F4 CHORD on its NOTE lane sets row B's kind, in order TRIAD (the default), 7TH, OPEN, POWER; SAMPLE has no key for it (F1 is the slot), and the right panel names
# the rows. Then PERFORM goes to TRACT (A NOTE, B TONGUE, C MOUTH) and SAMPLE still plays its own notes palette (11, 12). The WAV carries the notes; the unit's feel and
# the keys' LEDs by hand are for the unit.
