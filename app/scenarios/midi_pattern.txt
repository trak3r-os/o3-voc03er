# MIDI PATTERNS (M3): a program change picks the pattern at the pattern's end, and MIDI notes record into the PATTERN screen's lanes. Stopped, a
# note is step entry at the cursor (the cursor moves on); playing, it lands on the nearest sixteenth of the pattern that plays. A program above 7 is
# ignored. The PNGs show it; the unit check owed is an outside sequencer with FOLLOW on.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/midi_pattern.txt --card <fresh dir> --out <dir>   (the dirs must exist)
input tone 220 -8
wait 300
tap pattern
wait 100
snap 01_pattern_a
# stopped: three notes are three steps from the cursor, the velocity is the level
midi usb 90 3c 7f
wait 100
midi usb 90 3e 40
wait 100
midi usb 90 40 20
wait 200
snap 02_stopped_entry
# program change 5 (F) while stopped: the PATTERN screen shows pattern F, which lands at Play
midi usb c0 05
wait 200
snap 03_program_f
# a program above 7 is ignored
midi usb c0 20
wait 200
snap 04_program_ignored
tap play
wait 1200
# playing: a note lands on the step nearest to it in pattern F
midi usb 90 43 70
wait 1000
snap 05_recorded_playing
# program change 1 (B) at the pattern's end
midi usb c0 01
wait 3000
snap 06_program_b
tap play
wait 200
