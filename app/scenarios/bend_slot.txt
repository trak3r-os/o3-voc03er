# Bend moves a playing slot (SB1): a 1 bar take of a tone on the line is made in SAMPLE REC (AUTOPLAY OFF, then PERFORM's SOUNDS takes it: it is the carrier and plays at once, looped),
# then the MIDI wheel is pushed up, let go, pushed down, let go. The slot's speed follows Bend continuously (it used to play at 0.5x, 1x or 2x only).
# The simulator's input is synthetic: this shows that the bent slot sounds and the screen stays sane; the WAV carries the shift (the four wavs read 220, 246, 196 and 220 Hz at the end of each hold). The sound on real
# material, the click when the bend returns, and the CPU are for the unit.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/bend_slot.txt --out <dir>   (the dir must exist)
input tone 220 -8
wait 300
tap samplerec
wait 200
tap f3
tap f4              # AUTOPLAY OFF: the take waits in the slot
wait 100
tap play
wait 500
record on
tap rec
wait 9000
tap performance
wait 100

snap 02_wheel_up
wav bend_slot_up
midi usb e0 00 00
wait 800
snap 03_wheel_down
wav bend_slot_down
midi usb e0 00 40
wait 800
snap 04_wheel_centred
wav bend_slot_back
