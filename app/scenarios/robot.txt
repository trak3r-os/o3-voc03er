# ROBOT: the mode key cycles BANDS, TALK, TUNE, ROBOT; ROBOT draws the bars of BANDS and levels the voice with a fast compressor. The input
# tone steps 12 dB down and up, and the WAV shows how much of that comes out. The simulator feeds a synthetic tone: it shows the screen and
# the level behaviour, it cannot say how a voice sounds in ROBOT; that is by ear on the unit.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/robot.txt --out <dir>   (the dir must exist)
record on
input tone 220 -12
paddown 36
paddown 40
paddown 43
wait 600
snap 01_bands
tap f1
tap f1
wait 100
tap f1
wait 100
tap f1
wait 600
snap 02_robot
input tone 220 -24
wait 500
snap 03_robot_quiet
input tone 220 -12
wait 500
snap 04_robot_loud
input tone 220 -24
wait 500
input tone 220 -12
wait 500
tap f1
wait 600
snap 05_f1_stays_at_robot
padup 36
padup 40
padup 43
wait 300
wav robot
