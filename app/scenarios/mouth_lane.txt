# MOUTH LANE (TRACT V3, docs/REVIEW-2026-11-11.md section 4): PATTERN's fifth lane, the FX key (or UP / DOWN). In TRACT a chord on steps 1 to 8 (NOTE lane), then the
# MOUTH palette (the lower two rows): row A is the strip (the nine vowels A to U on pads 24..32, LIPS, TIP, BACK on 33..35), row B columns 0..8 are the same vowels with GLD (pads
# 36..44, a ">" before the cell), 45..47 dark. With REC on a palette press writes the cursor step and moves on (without REC it only sounds and becomes the hand); a step
# held plus a palette pad writes that step; SHIFT + a palette pad only sounds; SHIFT + a step clears it. The steps are the top two rows (pads 0..7 and 12..19). Played, the tube changes vowel at the steps (the WAV), a closure shuts it for the gate,
# and Stop opens it. Outside TRACT the lane is dim, still edits, and the strip says MOUTH SPEAKS IN TRACT once (snap 08). The simulator shows the screen,
# the LEDs and that the unit plays; the phrase by ear, the click at a slid step and the closures' sound are for the unit.
