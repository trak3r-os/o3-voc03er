# MOUTH LANE (TRACT V3, docs/REVIEW-2026-11-11.md section 4): PATTERN's fifth lane, F2 / the Fx key. In TRACT a chord on steps 1 to 8 (NOTE lane), then the
# MOUTH palette: row 0 is the strip (the nine vowels A to U on pads 0..8, LIPS, TIP, BACK on 9..11), row 1 columns 0..8 are the same vowels with GLD (pads
# 12..20, a ">" before the cell), 21..23 dark. A palette press writes the cursor step and moves on; a step held plus a palette pad writes that step;
# SHIFT + a palette pad only sounds; SHIFT + a step clears it. Played, the tube changes vowel at the steps (the WAV), a closure shuts it for the gate,
# and Stop opens it. Outside TRACT the lane is dim, still edits, and the strip says MOUTH SPEAKS IN TRACT once (snap 08). The simulator shows the screen,
# the LEDs and that the unit plays; the phrase by ear, the click at a slid step and the closures' sound are for the unit.
