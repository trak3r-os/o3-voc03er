# SAMPLE with one finger (rd-sam): BANDS, a 1 BAR take cut in eight, the slot speaks and LOOP is off. The WAV has four stretches, in this order: nothing held (silence: the tone in
# does not speak without a note), one top pad alone (the slice speaks: the pad brings the note, the root chord of row B, and it ends with the slice), one lower pad alone (its note and
# the picked slice, the last a top pad fired: slice 3 here), and the same lower pad after a top pad fired slice 6 (the picked slice follows the top pads).
# The simulator shows the screens and that the unit plays; how it feels under the fingers and the note's level against the slice are for the unit and the ear.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/sample_one_finger.txt --out <dir>   (the dir must exist)
