# The four voice loops on the PLAY screen: a tone at the line is recorded into loop 1 (REC, transport stopped so it starts at once),
# stopped, played as the modulator (UP) with the carrier chord held on the pads, the role swapped (DOWN), a second loop picked (NOTE).
# The simulator's input is synthetic: this shows the screen and that the loop path runs; it says nothing about how a loop sounds on a
# real source or what a playing loop costs on the unit.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/loops.txt --out <dir>   (the dir must exist)
