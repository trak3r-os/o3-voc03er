# U6a: pattern A of the bank, played by the step sequencer hook (stepseq.h). Five notes written on the PATTERN screen, a carrier on two
# steps (its CARRIER lane: SY, NZ, L1..L4), the transport started with SHIFT + PLAY, then stopped: the Stop must leave the user's
# CARRIER where it was (CarSrc is restored once, only because a step changed it). The simulator's input is synthetic: the WAV shows the
# notes follow the steps; how each carrier sounds is the unit's to say.
# Run: <build dir>/app/o3_voc03er_sim --scenario app/scenarios/stepseq.txt --out <dir>   (the dir must exist)
input tone 220 -12  # a modulator at the line jack, so the notes can be heard
record on
wait 200
tap pattern  # the PATTERN screen: palette pads write the cursor's step and move it on
wait 100
pad 0
pad 3
pad 7
pad 12
pad 15
expect state steps=5
tap left
tap left
tap left
tap left
tap left
tap instr  # the CARRIER lane: SYNTH on step 1, NOISE on step 3
pad 1
tap right
pad 2
snap 01_recorded
expect state carriers=2
press shift
tap play
release shift
wait 600
snap 02_running
expect state playing=1
wait 2400
snap 03_later
wav stepseq
press shift
tap play
release shift
wait 400
expect state playing=0
snap 04_stopped
