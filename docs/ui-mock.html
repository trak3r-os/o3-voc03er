VOC03ER: the screens in the skin

Drawn on 2026-11-06, before the screens were built, and kept as the design's first drawing. Where it and the app differ, the app is right (SONG, for one, is built now); the built screens are the PNGs of app/scenarios/.

Every main screen at 800 x 480, drawn with what the kit draws: flat fills, 1 px rules, round-cornered chips, discs and lines, alpha on shapes only, the four pixel faces. One rule: whatever speaks is the voice ink, whatever sounds is the carrier ink; a slot changes ink with its side. Under each screen the 48 pad LEDs as the screen lights them (monochrome: off, dim, low, mid, on). The type is your browser's monospace at the same cell sizes; on the unit it is the kit's pixel faces, crisper and squarer. The design is docs/UI-DESIGN.md.
